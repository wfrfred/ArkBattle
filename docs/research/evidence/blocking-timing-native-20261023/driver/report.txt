The question is answered in units of one BattleController.OnTick (a logical battle update), not a rendered frame. The native method pointer/metadata files are from the same installed client as blocking-native-20261023. Active XLua replacement delegates are not executed.

Boundary of conclusion
The raw direct-call scan of executable sections (.text and il2cpp) finds the above single calls to ObjectManager normal/late updates. A call-site scan alone does not rule out callback-induced explicit searches; sibling reports deliberately preserve those. Unity render-loop scheduling, all game-mode overrides, and active Lua replacements are not equated with one logical Tick in this report.
